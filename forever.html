4 NEW ZONES + EXPANDED WORLD - 1000+ NEW QUESTS

Zephras Isle - 1 to 12
Floating sky island, Skyborne Elf race start zone. Requires Skyborne Heroic Pack. Threat from outside, defend oasis. New race only zone.
Riverglades - 35 to 45
Brand-new zone size of STV, between Burning Steppes, Redridge, Swamp, Badlands. Powderfuse Port town. 150+ quests. Brotherhood of the Horse, Boulderok Ogre, Twilight's Hammer. Fixes awkward Classic leveling gap.
Shen'dralas - 40 to 45
Between Mulgore and Desolace. Highborne ruins, Eldre'Thalas / Dire Maul lore. Mordent Evenshade + Shen'dralar. Gelkis vs Magram centaur clans with faction rewards.
Mount Hyjal - 60
Proper Vanilla explorable after Archimonde defeat. Darkwhisper Gorge. Healing forests, Wild Gods, lingering corruption, Night Elves immortality sacrifice consequences. Endgame zone.

Plus expanded existing: Wetlands new quests, Desolace centaur faction quests, all starting zones new characters and profession hooks. Existing zones getting new stories throughout.

WoW Forever FAQ - Complete 2026

Do I need to buy Forever?
No, included with WoW sub or Game Time. But Skyborne race + Zephras Isle require Heroic Pack $30 or Epic $60 or Collection $80. Beta only with packs.
Is beta progress kept?
No. Beta wipe at launch. Everyone starts level 1 Nov 4.
What is Legacy System?
16 legacy points per char at launch account-wide. New progression horizontal, not level increase. Work across account for alts.
What about GDKP and Gold?
GDKP banned Sept 17 in Forever to protect guild social. Gold buying aggressively punished with permaban per Blizzard. We use hand-farmed small trades.
No dungeon finder?
Correct. Only LFG listing board with tags Learning / Relaxed / Competitive / Carry Offered. No WoW Token, no paid boost, carry is only skip.
What is fastest leveling?
Mage/Hunter 8% faster than average, Warrior 12% slower in Classic reference but Forever reworked all classes. RestedXP addon $30 faction $45 both. Our boost 12h/day per booster ~14 days 1-60.
