December 9, 2026 5 weeks after launch. All 3 same day. Spring 2027 2 more raids, Summer 2027 revamped iconic raid (likely Molten Core) + new raid.

Yes per Lead Classic Designer Tim Jones: every tier will have at least 10-man + 20-man. 40-man only exception launch. Promise for small and large guilds.

No. Story starts before classic raids. Returns later as revamped iconic raid Summer 2027. First is unnamed.
