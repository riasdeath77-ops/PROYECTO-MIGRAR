All Keystone Achievements FAQ - Season 2

1500 - Keystone Conqueror - What reward?
Title "The Venomous". Requires timed +7/+8 keys across all dungeons. First serious milestone.
2000 - Keystone Master - Mount?
Breath of Blight mount + Champion of the Dawn achievement. Unlocks all 8 dungeon teleports at timed +10. Most popular.
2500 - Keystone Hero - What is it?
2500 rating, sits between Master and Legend. Rewards Insidious Venomstone cosmetic for tier armor glow (Warband-wide). No mount, but includes Master mount.
3000 - Keystone Legend - Best mount?
Breath of Ruin mount - Midnight's main KSM mount. Requires timed +13/+14 keys. Includes Master + Hero rewards.
3600 - Keystone Myth - NEW in 12.1.5 - What?
NEW achievement from Patch 12.1.5. Attain 3600 rating. Rewards title "The Venomous Contender" + Timelost Saddle to buy old Master/Legend mounts. Requires timed +17/+18, only 4.3% of players hit it.
What above 3600?
Top 1% = Venomous Champion, Top 0.1% = Venomous Hero title "The Venomous Hero". Region-wide at season end, cannot be guaranteed - it's placement vs other players.
