WoW Forever PvP Boost - Rank 14 - Darkspear Islands 15v15 - Honor & Rank System

GearHavenGG Forever PvP Boost covers new PvP system revealed Oct 7 by Blizzard. Two tracks: Honor currency + Rank Points. Honor from killing enemy players, finishing battlegrounds, completing PvP quests. Rank Points from battlegrounds + PvP quests climb 14 ranks toward High Warlord Horde / Grand Marshal Alliance. Original 14 ranks return, weekly cap on Rank Points, so Rank 14 opens week 10 earliest. Honor cap 25,000 on beta.

NEW BATTLEGROUND - BATTLE FOR THE DARKSPEAR ISLANDS - 15v15 HYBRID

Location: Off coast of Dustwallow Marsh / Kalimdor. Levels 30 to 60. Scale between Warsong Gulch and Alterac Valley. 4 capture points spread across map generate points for holder, + flag spawns in center that can be returned to any base your team controls for extra point injection. First to 2,000 wins. Mix Eye of Storm + Arathi Basin mechanics. Holding more points = faster score rate. Flag adds burst points.

Queue: Battlemaster in capital cities or Barrens, or at Merchant Coast (Horde base) and Northwatch (Alliance base). Portals into BG at both.
Horde rep: Darkspear Raiders - trinkets, rings, cloaks, weapons, armor
Alliance rep: Theramore Expeditionary Force - same reward types
Extra: Exalted questline rewards faction tabard. Marks of Honor turn-in for rep gains. Holiday weeks bonus still.

RANK SYSTEM - 14 RANKS + HONOR SETS + LEGACY POINTS

Honor Gear
Honor buys blue and epic PvP sets. Blue to epic upgrade needs Emboldened Seal with rank requirement. Specs underserved getting new options: Shaman healer, melee, caster PvP sets reworked.
Rank Rewards
R1 tabard, R2 PvP trinket, then cloaks, necklaces, potions, battle standard, epic armor upgrades, Mount at Rank 11, Epic weapons at Rank 14
Weekly System
Rank Points weekly capped, less punishing than original but still ladder. Each season adds weekly journey tasks, 3 of them grant Legacy Point. Honor cap 25,000 beta.

Ruleset: PvP ruleset contested zones dangerous while leveling; Normal only by choice. Living character cannot switch rulesets later, choose once. Factions stay separate for groups, dungeons, raids. Both work for rank climb.

Forever PvP FAQ - Verified Oct 7

Is Rank 14 back?
Yes, original 14 ranks to Grand Marshal / High Warlord, confirmed Oct 7. Epic weapons at Rank 14, mount at Rank 11, weekly cap so Rank 14 earliest week 10 season.
How Darkspear scoring works?
4 points generate ongoing score, flag center to any base you control adds injection. Secure base before flag push. First to 2000 wins. 15v15, levels 30-60.
Can Horde and Alliance group together?
No. Factions separate for groups, dungeons, raids, and you group only with players on same ruleset (PvP vs Normal).
Is there arena?
Not announced. Confirmed: classic BGs, Darkspear Islands, PvP ruleset, 2027 season refreshes.
How to queue Darkspear?
Battlemaster in capitals or Barrens, or Merchant Coast Horde / Northwatch Alliance bases with portals + reputation vendors.
What boost offer?
Rank 1 to 14 boost, Honor farm 25k cap, Darkspear reputation Exalted + tabard, blue set, epic set with Seals, weekly journey + Legacy Points, mounts.
